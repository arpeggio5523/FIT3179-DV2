Top 10 Dog Breeds and Crossbreeds in South Australia

Compare the ten most common breed and crossbreed entries among dogs recorded as “At Home” in Dogs and Cats Online (DACO), as at 30 May 2025. Hover over a dot to see its exact value.

Australian Kelpie and Border Collie lead the list, with 18,107 and 17,353 dogs respectively. The gap between them is 754, compared with a gap of 4,289 between Border Collie and third-placed Labrador Retriever.

The top ten were selected from 2,738 breed and crossbreed entries, representing 331,557 dogs in total. Crossbreed entries are kept separate. “At Home” is a recorded DACO status, not a measure of new registrations during the year.

Source: Dog and Cat Management Board, Most Popular in SA 2025-05-30 , “Dog Breeds - SA” worksheet. Data extracted on 30 May 2025.

How have breed rankings changed in Townsville?

Follow changes in dog breed rankings across the March snapshots from 2023 to 2026. Six focus breeds are highlighted in colour, while grey lines show other breeds that reached the top 35 during this period.

Miniature Dachshund climbed from 32nd to 13th. Its recorded count increased from 268 in March 2023 to 460 in March 2026. Dachshund also improved its position, moving from 15th to 12th.

Maltese Terrier Cross remained first throughout the four snapshots. Staffordshire Bull Terrier fell from second to fourth, while Staffordshire Bull Terrier Cross rose from fourth to third. The two tied in 2025 before switching positions in 2026.

Source: Townsville City Council — Animal Registrations , March snapshots, 2023–2026. Counts describe registration records; changes do not necessarily represent equivalent changes in the actual dog population.

Where Is Owning More Than One Dog More Common?

Among recorded dog owners, 41.9% in Adelaide Plains had two or more dogs, compared with 12.5% in the City of Adelaide. Darker shading reveals where multiple-dog ownership is more common, rather than simply where more owners live.

Percentage = recorded owners with two or more dogs ÷ recorded owners with at least one dog × 100. These are owner records, not households or the percentage of all residents who own dogs. The data alone does not explain why ownership patterns differ.

Which Breeds Are Favourites Across the Most Councils?

Border Collies appear in the top five of 63 out of 68 councils—more than any other breed. Australian Kelpies and Maltese Crosses each appear in 47. This shows how widely breeds feature among local favourites, rather than how many dogs are recorded statewide.
